Emerging Global Security Signals

Civil-defence guidance, preparedness campaigns, exercises, official warnings, verified outages, new cyber advisories and policy shifts. The observed action is shown separately from AEGIS's interpretation; a signal does not by itself establish an imminent threat.

Methodology and Definitions

PriorityDefinition
CriticalActive severe consequences, significant confirmed activity, or potentially catastrophic disruption requiring urgent attention.
HighSignificant development or credible threat requiring heightened monitoring.
ModerateEmerging risk, preparedness initiative, or development requiring observation.
LowLimited immediate implications with potential intelligence relevance.
Evidence statusMeaning
ConfirmedSupported by primary evidence or at least two genuinely independent sources, and approved by an AEGIS editor.
ReportedAttributed to a named source but not independently verified.
DisputedCredible sources disagree; each account is shown with its attribution.
Analytical AssessmentAEGIS analysis of available evidence, labelled as analysis rather than fact.

AEGIS prefers primary documents, seeks at least two independent sources including affected-region reporting, and counts syndicated copies of the same report once. State-affiliated and Western official statements are treated alike, as attributed claims until supported by evidence. Updates and corrections are added to each assessment's history and never silently overwritten. Cyber vulnerabilities (CVEs, KEV, EPSS, patches) are covered in ThreatWatch and cross-linked from relevant events.
