iMethodology & disclaimer

ThreatWatch is generated each morning from public sources — CISA’s Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, the NVD, FIRST EPSS, Google Project Zero, ENISA EUVD, VulnCheck, Metasploit, Exploit-DB, PoC-in-GitHub and MITRE ATT&CK. Anything a source does not establish is stated as not established or not verified; nothing is estimated. Actor attribution appears only where MITRE ATT&CK cites it, or in sections an Aegis analyst has reviewed.

Priorities and response windows are guidance, not a substitute for your own risk assessment or policy. ThreatWatch never connects to, scans, or requests access to your systems. Corrections: info@aegisdefensecyber.com.
